Electrical Engineering · Colorado School of Mines · Class of 2027

I like working on technical problems where physical systems meet data.

I am an electrical engineering student whose research has moved through machine learning, signal processing, biomechanics, spectroscopy, robotics, embedded systems, heliophysics, and exoplanet science.

01

What I am working on now

I am currently a Research Assistant at NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory. My work includes a 1D convolutional neural network for detecting anomalous exoplanet transit signals in noisy light-curve data, along with software that identifies and categorizes solar-flare detection errors within the Excalibur pipeline.

02

Research across different fields

From May through August 2026, I conducted movement-biomechanics research at ETH Zürich, developing a MATLAB-based modeling pipeline that uses wearable IMU data to estimate foot placement and evaluate gait stability.

Before that, at NASA Goddard Space Flight Center, I processed H-Lyman-alpha intensity data using JupyterHub and MATLAB and worked with a 28-layer residual neural network to model emission patterns for exospheric mapping research.

What connects it all

I am most interested in research where engineering tools can make complex scientific data easier to understand, validate, and use. I especially like projects that combine a real physical system with data-driven modeling.
